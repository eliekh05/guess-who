How to play

  1. Each player secretly picks one of the 24 characters.
  2. Take turns asking YES/NO questions about physical features.
  3. Flip down characters that don't match the answers.
  4. When you're confident, guess who their character is!
  5. ⚠️ A wrong guess means you lose instantly.
